About the Society

Building an economics culture at Bharati College.

EconBharati aims to provide a common academic space where students can connect economic theory with real-world issues, develop analytical skills and participate in research-oriented activities.

“Economics is not only about understanding markets — it is about understanding choices, institutions and society.”
What We Do

Learn. Research. Participate.

Activities can be expanded throughout the academic year based on student interests and faculty guidance.
